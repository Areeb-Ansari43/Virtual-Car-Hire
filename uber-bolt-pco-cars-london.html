What Cars Can You Drive on Uber and Bolt in London?

A detailed breakdown of Transport for London (TfL) regulations, platform categories (UberX, Comfort, Exec, XL), Zero Emission rules, and compliant PCO cars available for hire.

9 min read Updated March 2026 Written by Virtual Car Hire Editorial Team

Key Rule: TfL Licensing vs Platform Eligibility

In Greater London, every vehicle used for private hire work must first hold an active TfL PHV Licence Disc. Once licensed by TfL, a vehicle's acceptance on platforms like Uber and Bolt depends on the platform's specific category requirements, model age, seating capacity, and fuel/emissions type. Requirements can change periodically, so verifying current operator lists is always recommended.

TfL Zero Emission Capability (ZEC) Rules

To reduce air pollution in London, Transport for London enforces strict Zero Emission Capability (ZEC) rules for private hire vehicles:

  • Newly Licensed Vehicles: All vehicles licensed for the first time as a PHV in London must be Zero Emission Capable (ZEC), meeting minimum electric-only range and CO2 emission thresholds (or full battery electric).
  • Existing Licensed Vehicles: Hybrid vehicles that held a valid TfL PHV licence prior to recent rule updates may continue operating until their maximum age limit (typically up to 10–12 years from registration, subject to annual TfL inspection).

Uber and Bolt Ride Categories Explained

1. Standard Tiers: UberX & Bolt Standard

These entry-level categories account for the highest volume of trip requests across London. Suitable vehicles are 4-door, 5-seater hatchbacks, saloons, or estates that offer reliability and low operating expenses.

Popular choices include:

  • Toyota Corolla Estate — Reliable hybrid with exceptional fuel economy and huge boot capacity for airport transfers.
  • Toyota Prius — The classic, proven hybrid choice for urban London driving.
  • Toyota Auris Estate — Highly economical hybrid estate with vast luggage room.
  • Hyundai IONIQ — Smooth hybrid saloon offering excellent MPG and passenger comfort.
  • MG5 EV — Fully electric estate car with generous range and zero tailpipe emissions.

2. Green Tiers: Uber Green & Bolt Green

Reserved exclusively for 100% zero-emission battery electric vehicles (BEVs). Drivers operating on electric tiers benefit from reduced operator commission rates, higher passenger demand, Congestion Charge exemption, and access to zero-emission zones.

Top electric PCO models available on Virtual Car Hire include:

  • Tesla Model 3 — London's premier electric PCO car, eligible for UberX, Uber Green, and Uber Comfort.
  • Mercedes EQE — Luxury executive EV providing long range, state-of-the-art tech, and premium fares.
  • Mercedes EQS — Ultra-luxury electric saloon for executive chauffeur and VIP requests.
  • Jaguar I-Pace — All-electric performance SUV combining style, range, and luxury.
  • MG5 EV — Cost-effective electric estate ideal for high-mileage drivers.

3. Comfort & Executive Tiers: Uber Comfort, Uber Exec & Bolt Executive

Premium tiers command higher per-mile fares. Requirements include newer vehicle model years, extra legroom, leather interior trim, and higher driver ratings.

Top Executive & Comfort choices on Virtual Car Hire:

  • Mercedes EQE & Mercedes EQS — Leading zero-emission executive cars.
  • Mercedes E220 & Mercedes E300 — The benchmark executive saloons for corporate chauffeur work.
  • Tesla Model 3 — Qualifies for Uber Comfort with spacious seating and instant electric acceleration.
  • MG S9 PHEV SUV & Hyundai Santa Fe — Premium SUVs offering commanding ride height and extra legroom.

4. Large Passenger Tiers: UberXL, Uber Exec Van & Bolt XL

Multi-purpose vehicles (MPVs) seating 6 to 8 passengers cater to group bookings, airport runs to Heathrow and Gatwick, and corporate roadshows.

Top MPVs available on Virtual Car Hire:

  • Mercedes V-Class — Luxury 7/8-seater MPV for high-end executive group travel.
  • Mercedes Vito — Versatile 8/9-seater private hire van with massive luggage space.
  • Ford Tourneo Custom — Extremely spacious MPV ideal for airport passenger transport.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the basic vehicle requirements for Uber and Bolt in London?

Vehicles must be TfL-licensed as a PHV, 4-door, seat at least 4 passengers, meet current emissions rules, and be covered by Hire & Reward insurance.

Can I drive a hybrid car on Uber or Bolt in London?

Yes. Existing TfL-licensed hybrid cars carrying valid PHV discs (like the Toyota Corolla Estate or Toyota Prius) are fully eligible for Uber and Bolt standard categories.

Which cars qualify for Uber Exec or Uber Comfort?

Executive and Comfort categories accept high-spec saloons and EVs with premium interiors and ample legroom, such as the Mercedes EQE, Mercedes EQS, Mercedes E220, and Tesla Model 3.

Are electric cars cheaper to operate on Uber and Bolt?

Yes. Fully electric cars benefit from lower per-mile fuel costs, lower platform service fees on Uber Green, exemption from Congestion Charges, and higher customer satisfaction scores.
