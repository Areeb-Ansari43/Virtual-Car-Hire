PCO Car Hire Tax Deductible Expenses in London (2026 HMRC Guide)

A clear breakdown of allowable self-employed expenses for London private hire drivers: PCO rental payments, fuel, EV charging, platform fees, licensing, and maximizing tax refunds.

Self-Employed PCO Taxation Essentials

As a professional private hire driver in London operating via Uber, Bolt, Free Now, or independent operator accounts, you are classified by HMRC as a self-employed sole trader. Understanding allowable business expenses is essential to reducing your taxable profit legally and keeping more of your hard-earned income.

1. PCO Car Hire as an Allowable Expense

Under HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) guidelines, the full cost of renting a commercial private hire vehicle used for trade is 100% tax-deductible. When you rent a vehicle from Virtual Car Hire, every weekly invoice you receive serves as a valid business expense proof.

2. Tax Advantages of Renting vs Purchasing

While purchasing a new electric or hybrid car outright involves complex capital allowance depreciation calculations (and potential capital losses when selling), PCO car rental simplifies your accounting:

  • Flat Direct Expense: Your weekly rental payment is written off immediately in the tax year it is paid, reducing your Self Assessment bill without multi-year depreciation schedules.
  • No Hidden Maintenance Bills: Routine servicing, tyre replacements, MOT testing, and roadside cover are managed by Virtual Car Hire, eliminating unexpected repair costs.
  • Clear Invoicing: Invoices are readily accessible via your driver portal, ready for your accountant or tax software.

4. Best Practices for HMRC Record Keeping

HMRC requires self-employed sole traders to retain business financial records for a minimum of 5 years following the 31 January submission deadline. Follow these tips:

  1. Dedicated Business Bank Account: Keep your PCO income and vehicle expenses strictly separate from personal household spending.
  2. Digital Receipt Capture: Use smartphone apps to photograph fuel receipts, car wash expenses, and parking tickets immediately.
  3. Download Weekly Invoices: Access your Virtual Car Hire portal regularly to archive weekly rental invoices for your accountant.

Is weekly PCO car hire tax deductible with HMRC?

Yes. 100% of your weekly vehicle rental payments to Virtual Car Hire are allowable business expenses that reduce your taxable income on your annual Self Assessment return.

Can I claim EV charging and fuel as a tax expense?

Yes. Fuel purchases and commercial public EV charging costs incurred for business driving are fully tax deductible under HMRC rules.
