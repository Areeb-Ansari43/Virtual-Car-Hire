How to Start Driving for Uber or Bolt in London: A Beginner's Guide

A practical, step-by-step roadmap for new private hire drivers in London and Luton: from getting your TfL PCO licence to selecting a vehicle, creating app accounts, and earning your first payout.

Last reviewed: October 2026 10 min read Last Verified: March 2026 · TfL Compliant Written by Virtual Car Hire Editorial Team

Executive Summary: The 3 Core Requirements

To start accepting passengers on Uber or Bolt in London, you need three core elements:

  1. A valid TfL PHV Driver Licence (PCO badge).
  2. An eligible TfL-licensed PCO Vehicle with valid Hire & Reward insurance.
  3. An active Driver Account registered on Uber, Bolt, or Free Now.

Step 1: Obtain Your TfL PCO Driver Badge

Before registering on ride-hailing apps, you must hold a valid Transport for London (TfL) Private Hire Vehicle driver badge. If you haven't applied yet, refer to our detailed guide on How to Get a PCO Licence in London.

Key prerequisites for your driver application include:

  • Minimum age of 21 and a full UK driving licence held for at least 3 years.
  • Enhanced DBS criminal record check registered on the Update Service.
  • DVLA Group 2 medical assessment (Form TPH/204).
  • Passing TfL's SERU assessment and English Language Requirement.

Step 2: Choose an Eligible PCO Vehicle

Once your PCO driver badge arrives, you need a compliant vehicle. Driving your personal car is rarely possible because TfL requires every private-hire car to hold an official PHV Licence Disc and specialized commercial Hire & Reward insurance.

Instead of purchasing a new car outright, most new drivers choose PCO car hire. Hiring provides instant access to a fully licensed vehicle without large capital investment, depreciation risks, or surprise repair bills.

Vehicle Selection Tips

Matching Your Vehicle to Your Driving Goals

  • High Mileage Urban Driving: Electric models like the Tesla Model 3 or MG5 EV qualify for Uber Green, offer lower charge costs, and eliminate Congestion Charge fees.
  • Airport & Long-Distance Routes: Hybrid estates like the Toyota Corolla Estate provide exceptional fuel economy and huge boot capacity for luggage.
  • Executive Passenger Services: Saloons like the Mercedes EQE or Mercedes E220 unlock higher-fare Uber Exec and Bolt Executive requests.

Step 3: Register Your Operator Accounts (Uber, Bolt, Free Now)

Download the Uber Driver and Bolt Driver apps and create your accounts. You will upload:

  1. Your UK photocard driving licence and TfL PHV driver badge.
  2. Your bank account details for weekly direct deposits.
  3. Your vehicle documents (supplied directly by Virtual Car Hire), including the vehicle V5C logbook, TfL PHV licence disc certificate, and Hire & Reward insurance cover note.

At Virtual Car Hire, our team assists you with uploading these vehicle documents directly to Uber and Bolt, ensuring rapid approval—often within 24 to 48 hours.

Step 4: Going Online & Maximizing Your Earnings

Once your documents are verified, toggle your driver app to "Online" and start accepting trips. Here are four pro tips for beginner drivers in London:

  • Run Multiple Apps Simultaneously: Keep both Uber and Bolt online while waiting for jobs. Accept the first good ride and go offline on the other app until the trip completes.
  • Focus on High-Demand Hours: Peak earnings occur during weekday morning commutes (06:00–09:30), evening peak hours (16:30–19:30), and weekend evenings around West End entertainment hubs and London airports (Heathrow, Gatwick, Luton).
  • Maintain High Ratings: Keep your car spotless, offer phone charging cables, drive smoothly, and remain polite. High ratings keep you eligible for Uber Comfort and executive requests.
  • Manage Fuel & Charging Expenses: Charge electric vehicles overnight during off-peak energy rates or utilize rapid charging hubs efficiently between shifts.
