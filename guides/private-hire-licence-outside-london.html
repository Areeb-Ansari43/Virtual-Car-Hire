Private Hire Licensing Outside London: A UK Driver's Guide (2026)

Outside London, private hire is licensed by local councils, and every council sets its own rules. Here is how it works across the UK in 2026, and what to do if you want to drive in London.

Last reviewed: November 2026 9 min read Written by the Virtual Car Hire team

Who licenses private hire outside London

In London, TfL licenses drivers, vehicles and operators. Everywhere else in England and Wales, it is the local council for the area. Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own systems. You usually apply to the council where you plan to work, and each one sets its own tests, fees and licence lengths.

What councils usually ask for

  • A full driving licence, held for a minimum period. Councils differ: some ask for 12 months, others three years.
  • The right to work in the UK. Councils must refuse a licence without it.
  • An enhanced DBS check, often with the DBS Update Service, to show you are a fit and proper person.
  • A medical to Group 2 standard, which often includes an eyesight test.
  • A knowledge or local area test, sometimes with English, maths and Highway Code questions.
  • A driving assessment or training course, required by many councils.

Fees vary a lot. As one example, West Norfolk lists £192 for a one-year combined licence, with the DBS and knowledge test charged separately. Always check your own council's website.

Out-of-area working and Wolverhampton plates

Many private hire drivers licensed by one council do most of their work somewhere else. Wolverhampton is the best-known example: reports in 2026 said it licensed almost 38,000 private hire vehicles, many driven far outside the city.

MPs on the Transport Committee have called out-of-area working a widespread feature of the market, and the government has said it wants to reduce it, with plans reported to include national minimum standards, a national licensing database and stronger enforcement. Other councils have also given Wolverhampton officers enforcement powers. The final details were not settled at the time of writing, so check gov.uk for the latest.

Want to drive in London?

To work for London operators you need a TfL private hire driver licence and a TfL-licensed vehicle. A council licence from elsewhere does not transfer. Our step-by-step PCO licence guide covers the TfL process, including the SERU and topographical assessments.

Once you have your TfL licence, every car we hire out is already TfL licensed. We also supply drivers around Luton and Heathrow from our Hounslow depot.

Frequently asked questions

Who issues private hire licences outside London?

Your local council. Each council sets its own requirements, tests and fees.

Can I drive in London with a council private hire licence?

To work for London operators you need a TfL private hire driver licence and a TfL-licensed vehicle.

Why are so many cars licensed in Wolverhampton?

Many drivers licensed by Wolverhampton work in other areas. The government has said it wants to reduce out-of-area working through national reforms.

How long does a council private hire licence last?

It depends on the council. Some issue one-year licences and others three-year licences.
