PCO Car Insurance Explained: Cover and Costs (2026)

Insurance is one of the biggest costs of private hire driving, and one of the most confusing. Here is what cover you need, what it typically costs, and the options that keep it simple.

Last reviewed: November 2026 8 min read Written by the Virtual Car Hire team

What PCO insurance is

Hire and reward cover

PCO insurance, also called private hire or minicab insurance, is motor insurance that covers carrying paying passengers. This is known as hire and reward cover. A normal car insurance policy specifically excludes it, so driving for Uber, Bolt or any operator on a standard policy leaves you uninsured.

For private hire work you need hire and reward cover for the vehicle you are driving. Operators check insurance documents before you can go online, and TfL licensing relies on it too.

The main types of cover

  • Private hire insurance is for pre-booked work through an operator such as Uber or Bolt. It is usually cheaper than public hire.
  • Public hire insurance is for black cabs and hackney carriages that can be hailed in the street.
  • Annual policies are usually cheapest over a full year if you drive full time.
  • Monthly or 30-day policies suit drivers who are starting out or working part of the year.
  • Top-up policies add hire and reward cover on top of your personal insurance while you are working.

Uber also provides some cover to drivers through its partner protection scheme, but it is not a substitute for your own hire and reward policy. Read what it covers before you rely on it.

What it costs in 2026

Prices vary widely by driver and car. As a guide, one 2026 industry estimate puts comprehensive private hire insurance for a London driver with a clean record at around £2,600 to £3,400 a year, roughly £50 to £65 a week. Newer drivers, recent claims and high-value cars push it higher.

Ways to keep insurance costs down

  • Keep a clean record. Every claim and point shows up in your premium for years.
  • Compare annual and monthly prices once you know how long you will drive.
  • Choose a car that suits your work, not the most expensive one you can get.
  • Check whether a rental with insurance included works out cheaper than your own car and policy. Our rent vs buy guide runs through it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use normal car insurance for Uber?

No. Standard policies exclude hire and reward. You need private hire insurance to carry paying passengers.

How much is PCO insurance in London?

One 2026 industry estimate puts it at around £2,600 to £3,400 a year for a driver with a clean record. Your quote depends on your age, record, car and mileage.

Is insurance included when I hire a PCO car?

Is Uber's own insurance enough?

No. Uber's partner protection is extra cover, not a replacement for your own hire and reward policy.
