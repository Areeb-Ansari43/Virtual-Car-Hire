How to Get a PCO Licence in London: Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Everything you need to know about applying for a Transport for London (TfL) private hire driver licence, from medical checks and DBS applications to SERU tests and securing your first PCO car.

10 min read Updated March 2026 Written by Virtual Car Hire Editorial Team

What is a PCO Licence?

A PCO licence (officially named a TfL Private Hire Vehicle (PHV) Driver Licence) is the official accreditation issued by Transport for London that permits individuals to carry passengers for hire and reward across Greater London. Whether you plan to drive for ride-hailing apps like Uber, Bolt, Ola, and Free Now, or work with local private-hire firms, holding a valid TfL PCO licence is mandatory.

TfL PCO Driver Eligibility Requirements

Before beginning your application on the official TfL Private Hire portal, you must satisfy all basic regulatory eligibility criteria:

  • Age Limit: You must be at least 21 years old at the time of application.
  • Driving Experience: You must hold a full UK, EU, or EEA driving licence that has been valid for at least 3 years.
  • Right to Work: You must possess legal entitlement to work in the United Kingdom.
  • Character & Conduct: You must pass an Enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) criminal record check.
  • Medical Standard: You must meet the DVLA Group 2 medical standard as confirmed by a registered doctor.
  • Assessments: You must successfully complete TfL's Safety, Equality and Regulatory Understanding (SERU) assessment and satisfy the English Language Requirement (ELR).

The Step-by-Step Application Process

Step 1

Obtain an Enhanced DBS Check

Your background check must be processed through TfL's official contracted supplier, First Advantage. It is vital to register your DBS certificate with the DBS Update Service within 30 days of issue. Failing to subscribe to the Update Service means your application could be delayed or require a fresh DBS check.

Step 2

Complete Your Medical Examination (TPH/204 Form)

You must undergo a medical assessment using the official TfL TPH/204 form. This form must be completed and signed by a GP or registered medical practitioner who has full access to your medical history records. The medical verifies that your vision, cardiovascular health, and general physical fitness meet DVLA Group 2 commercial standards.

Step 3

Submit Your Online TfL Application

Register an account on the TfL Private Hire portal. You will need to upload copies of:

  • Your UK photocard driving licence and DVLA check code.
  • Your completed TPH/204 medical form.
  • Your Enhanced DBS check reference.
  • Proof of address (such as a bank statement or utility bill less than 3 months old).
  • Proof of your right to work in the UK (e.g. British passport or Share Code).
  • A passport-style digital photograph meeting TfL specifications.
Step 4

Pass the SERU and English Language Assessments

TfL requires all new applicants to attend a driver assessment center to complete two evaluations:

  1. SERU Assessment: Tests your understanding of safety duties, passenger equality, road safety, and regulatory duties as a private hire driver in London.
  2. English Language Requirement (ELR): Evaluates your speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills to ensure effective communication with passengers and emergency services.

Estimated Timescales and Application Costs

While exact processing times depend on TfL workload and background check turnaround times, most applicants complete the process within 12 to 16 weeks. Below is a breakdown of average costs associated with getting licensed:

What Happens After Receiving Your PCO Badge?

Once TfL approves your application, you will receive your physical PHV driver badge and paper licence by recorded post. With your badge in hand, you can:

  1. Sign up with major ride-hailing platforms like Uber, Bolt, Ola, and Free Now.
  2. Choose a compliant PCO vehicle to start working.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PCO licence?

A PCO licence is the official Transport for London (TfL) Private Hire Vehicle driver licence. It grants you legal authorization to carry passengers for payment in private hire vehicles across London.

How long does it take to get a PCO licence in London?

The process generally takes between 12 to 16 weeks, depending on how quickly your DBS check is returned, medical records are verified, and SERU assessment appointments are scheduled.

How much does a PCO licence cost in London?

Total official fees amount to roughly £460 to £520, covering the TfL fee (£310), Enhanced DBS (£50-£60), medical form (£50-£100), and SERU exam (£36).

Do I need my own car to apply for a PCO licence?

No. Driver licensing is entirely separate from vehicle licensing. You can apply for your badge first, and then hire an active, TfL-licensed vehicle from Virtual Car Hire once your badge arrives.
