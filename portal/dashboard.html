Vehicle Maintenance & Reminders

Welcome to Virtual Car Hire! Please acknowledge the following vehicle management notification terms:

  • By default, you are enrolled in automated email reminders for MOT, routine servicing, and PCO licence renewals.
  • Once your vehicle hire contract ends, these automated reminder notifications stop automatically.
  • If you return as an active driver at a later date, you will be automatically re-enrolled to keep your vehicle compliant.
