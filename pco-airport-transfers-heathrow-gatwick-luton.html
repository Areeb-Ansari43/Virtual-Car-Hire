PCO Airport Transfers: Heathrow, Gatwick, Luton & Stansted

Master the logistics of London airport transfer driving: terminal drop-off rules, staging areas, avoiding PCN fines, and picking the ultimate luggage-friendly PCO car.

Last reviewed: October 2026 11 min read Last Verified: March 2026 · Airport Compliant Written by Virtual Car Hire Editorial Team

The Value of Airport Transfers for PCO Drivers

Airport transfer jobs are among the most lucrative trips for London private hire drivers. Journeys connecting central London with major international hubs—such as London Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Luton (LTN), and Stansted (STN)—offer long mileage, steady off-peak demand, premium passenger tips, and high job completion predictability.

Terminal Drop-Off Charges & Staging Rules

Every major airport surrounding Greater London enforces strict barrier or ANPR camera-controlled terminal drop-off systems. Understanding charges and setting up automated accounts is vital to avoid costly Penalty Charge Notices (PCNs):

Airport Drop-off Fee Payment Mechanism Holding / Staging Area
Heathrow Airport (LHR) £5.00 APCOA Online or AutoPay account Authorised Vehicle Area (AVA) on Northern Perimeter Rd
Gatwick Airport (LGW) £5.00 NCP / Gatwick Airport drop-off portal Long Stay Car Park virtual queue holding zone
Luton Airport (LTN) £5.00 (up to 10 mins) Barrier payment / AutoPay account Mid Stay Car Park / Express Drop-off lane
Stansted Airport (STN) £7.00 (up to 15 mins) Express Drop-Off zone barrier / online Birchanger Green Services or official PHV holding zone

How Staging and AVA Queueing Works

When dropping off passengers at terminal forecourts, drivers cannot linger or wait for new inbound job dispatches. Doing so triggers immediate security dispersal or automated CCTV PCNs. Instead, follow platform protocols:

Heathrow

London Heathrow AVA Zone Protocol

At Heathrow Airport, ride-hailing apps enforce virtual queueing inside the Authorised Vehicle Area (AVA) located on Northern Perimeter Road. When you enter the AVA zone, your app registers your position in line. Once a arriving passenger books a ride, you are dispatched directly to the short-stay car park pickup plaza (e.g. Terminal 2, 3, 4, or 5 Short Stay).

Luton & Gatwick

Serving London Luton and Gatwick Airports

For drivers serving Luton, Virtual Car Hire provides localized support via our Luton PCO car hire service. When picking up at Luton Airport, drivers utilize designated express pickup car parks or short-stay bays, ensuring quick passenger meet-and-greet with clear luggage loading access.

Choosing the Ultimate Airport Transfer Vehicle

Airport passengers frequently travel with multiple large check-in suitcases and hand luggage. Small hatchbacks with restricted boot space (under 350 litres) risk passenger rejection or uncomfortable cabin crowding. Key requirements for airport PCO cars include:

  • Generous Luggage Volume: Minimum 450 to 600+ litres of rear boot capacity to accommodate 3–4 large flight suitcases simultaneously.
  • Highway Refinement & Efficiency: Excellent fuel efficiency or high EV battery range during high-speed M4, M25, M1, and M11 motorway cruising.
  • Passenger Rear Comfort: Ample rear legroom and USB charging ports for arriving international tourists and business executives.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do terminal drop-off charges work at Heathrow and Gatwick?

Heathrow and Gatwick charge £5 per terminal drop-off. Ride-hailing apps usually add this charge to the fare automatically, but drivers must ensure auto-pay is set up on official portals to prevent PCN fines.

Which PCO vehicle is best for airport luggage?

Estate vehicles like the Toyota Corolla Estate or MG5 EV are ideal because they provide expansive boot space for 3 to 4 large suitcases.

Where do drivers wait for Heathrow pickups?

Drivers must wait inside Heathrow's official Authorised Vehicle Area (AVA) on Northern Perimeter Road until dispatched to a terminal short-stay car park by their app.
