Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Virtual Car Hire Ltd ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Driving licence and PCO badge details for hire purposes
  • Payment information processed securely through our payment providers
  • Usage data and cookies from our website

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your vehicle hire application and manage your account
  • Communicate with you about your hire agreement
  • Upload your documents to ride-hailing platforms (Uber, Bolt, Ola) on your behalf
  • Improve our services and website
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@fa-ibi.co.uk.
