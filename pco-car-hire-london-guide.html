PCO Car Hire in London: What Drivers Need to Know Before Hiring a Car

An insider guide for private hire drivers in London and Luton: comparing PCO car hire vs retail rental, understanding insurance terms, avoiding hidden fees, and checking vehicles prior to collection.

Last reviewed: October 2026 8 min read Last Verified: March 2026 · TfL Compliant Written by Virtual Car Hire Editorial Team

What is PCO Car Hire?

PCO Car Hire is a specialized commercial vehicle rental service tailored explicitly for licensed private-hire drivers operating in Greater London and surrounding regions like Luton. Unlike standard car hire, a PCO hire vehicle is pre-licensed with official Transport for London (TfL) PHV discs and supplied with commercial Hire & Reward motor insurance, allowing you to start earning on Uber, Bolt, and Free Now legally and immediately.

Key Considerations Before Choosing a PCO Rental Company

1. Comprehensive Hire & Reward Insurance

Driving for payment without specific Hire & Reward insurance is illegal and carries 6 to 8 penalty points on your driving licence. Ensure your PCO rental provider includes comprehensive Hire & Reward insurance in the advertised weekly rate, with transparent excess details.

2. Routine Servicing, MOT & Wear and Tear

Private-hire vehicles in London average between 700 to 1,200 miles per week. Make sure your agreement covers scheduled mechanical servicing, brake pad replacements, annual TfL MOT checks, and roadside breakdown assistance without extra hidden costs.

3. Monthly Mileage Allowance

Some rental companies impose restrictive weekly caps (e.g., 600 miles/week) and charge steep fees per extra mile. At Virtual Car Hire, drivers benefit from a generous 5,000 miles per month allowance (over 1,150 miles weekly), giving you complete freedom for full-time shifts and airport runs.

4. Refundable Deposit and Transparent Contract Terms

Check the required deposit amount upfront and confirm the vehicle return conditions. Reputable hire companies hold deposits securely and offer fair 1-to-2 week notice periods if you decide to hand back the vehicle or upgrade to a newer model.

Documents Needed When Collecting Your PCO Rental

To collect a PCO vehicle and get approved on ride-hailing apps, you will need to present:

  • UK photocard driving licence and DVLA check code.
  • Active TfL PHV driver badge and paper counterpart licence.
  • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement issued within the last 3 months).
  • National Insurance number.
  • Refundable deposit and initial week's rental payment.

Vehicle Inspection Checklist Before Driving Away

When collecting your rental car at our West London office (or Luton location), take 5 minutes to conduct a joint walkaround inspection with our team:

  1. Exterior Bodywork & Wheels: Inspect all body panels, bumpers, and alloy wheels. Ensure any minor pre-existing marks are recorded on the handover form.
  2. Tyre Depth & Condition: Verify that all four tyres have healthy tread (well above the legal 1.6mm limit) and proper inflation.
  3. Windscreen & TfL Discs: Check that the front windscreen has no cracks in the driver's line of sight and that official TfL PHV licence discs are affixed to both front and rear glass.
  4. In-Car Documentation: Confirm that copies of the vehicle logbook (V5C), TfL PHV licence, MOT certificate, and insurance cover note are present in the glovebox.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between normal car hire and PCO car hire?

Normal retail car hire provides standard personal insurance that strictly prohibits commercial passenger transport. PCO car hire includes a TfL-licensed vehicle fitted with valid PHV discs and specialized Hire & Reward insurance required for Uber, Bolt, and private hire circuits.

What is included in Virtual Car Hire's weekly rental rate?

Every rental includes comprehensive Hire & Reward insurance, routine servicing, MOT tests, roadside breakdown cover, 5,000 miles monthly allowance, and platform document support.

What documents do I need to present when hiring a PCO vehicle?

You need your UK driving licence, DVLA check code, TfL PHV driver badge, proof of address (less than 3 months old), and National Insurance number.

What should I check before driving away with a rental PCO car?

Perform a walkaround visual inspection to check bodywork, tyre tread depth, fluid levels, windscreen condition, valid TfL PHV discs, and glovebox paperwork.
