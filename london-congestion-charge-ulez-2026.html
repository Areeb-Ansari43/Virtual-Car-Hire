London Congestion Charge & ULEZ Rules for PCO Drivers (2026 Guide)

Everything London private hire drivers need to know about navigating central London charges, Ultra Low Emission Zone rules, the 2026 electric car discount, and EV cost savings.

Last reviewed: November 2026 8 min read Last Verified: March 2026 · TfL Compliant Written by Virtual Car Hire Editorial Team

Key London Environmental Zones

Transport for London operates two primary low-emission charging schemes in Greater London: the Congestion Charge Zone (CCZ) covering central London, and the expanded Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) encompassing all 32 London boroughs. Understanding how these zones apply to private hire vehicles is critical to maintaining profitability.

1. London Congestion Charge Zone (CCZ)

The Congestion Charge is a daily £18 fee operating between 07:00 and 18:00 Monday to Friday, and 12:00 to 18:00 Saturday, Sunday, and Bank Holidays (excluding the period between Christmas Day and New Year's Day Bank Holiday inclusive).

Vehicle Category Congestion Charge Status Weekly Expense
100% Zero-Emission EV (Tesla Model 3, MG5 EV) 25% discount (£13.50 a day) when registered on TfL Auto Pay Up to £67.50 / week
Plug-in Hybrid (PHEV) (Mercedes E300e) Full daily £18 charge in the central zone Up to £90–£126 / week
Self-Charging Hybrid (Toyota Corolla Estate) Full daily £18 charge in the central zone Up to £90–£126 / week

Note: Ride-hailing platforms like Uber and Bolt automatically add a Congestion Charge surcharge to passenger fares when a ride begins or ends inside the CCZ. However, driving through the zone without an active passenger journey remains the driver's expense.

2. Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) Regulations

The Ultra Low Emission Zone applies 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year across all London boroughs within the M25 boundary. Non-compliant vehicles face a daily fee of £12.50.

The 2026 electric car discount (Auto Pay)

The old Cleaner Vehicle Discount, which let fully electric cars drive in the zone for free, ended on 25 December 2025. Since 2 January 2026, fully electric cars (such as our Tesla Model 3 or MG5 EV) get a 25% discount, paying £13.50 a day instead of £18, but only if the vehicle is registered on TfL Auto Pay. If it is not on Auto Pay, you pay the full £18.

Private hire vehicles have not had a general exemption from the Congestion Charge since April 2019. Wheelchair-accessible PHVs keep an exemption, but only while carrying out a booking for a TfL-licensed operator. Always check the current rules on TfL's PHV Congestion Charge page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do PCO drivers pay the London Congestion Charge?

Yes. Private hire drivers pay the daily £18 charge when driving in the zone during charging hours. Fully electric cars registered on TfL Auto Pay get a 25% discount and pay £13.50. The old free Cleaner Vehicle Discount ended on 25 December 2025.

Are hybrid cars exempt from the Congestion Charge?

No. Hybrids and plug-in hybrids pay the full £18 daily charge. Only fully electric cars get the 25% Auto Pay discount, and no car type is fully exempt any more.

Are all Virtual Car Hire vehicles ULEZ compliant?

Yes. 100% of the Virtual Car Hire fleet meets ULEZ standards, ensuring you never pay daily ULEZ charges anywhere in Greater London.
