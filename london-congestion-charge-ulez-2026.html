London Congestion Charge & ULEZ Rules for PCO Drivers (2026 Guide)

Everything London private hire drivers need to know about navigating central London charges, Ultra Low Emission Zone rules, Cleaner Vehicle Discounts, and EV cost savings.

Last reviewed: October 2026 8 min read Last Verified: March 2026 · TfL Compliant Written by Virtual Car Hire Editorial Team

Key London Environmental Zones

Transport for London operates two primary low-emission charging schemes in Greater London: the Congestion Charge Zone (CCZ) covering central London, and the expanded Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) encompassing all 32 London boroughs. Understanding how these zones apply to private hire vehicles is critical to maintaining profitability.

1. London Congestion Charge Zone (CCZ)

The Congestion Charge is a daily £15.00 fee operating between 07:00 and 18:00 Monday to Friday, and 12:00 to 18:00 Saturday, Sunday, and Bank Holidays (excluding the period between Christmas Day and New Year's Day Bank Holiday inclusive).

Vehicle Category Congestion Charge Status Weekly Expense
100% Zero-Emission EV (Tesla Model 3, MG5 EV) Exempt via Cleaner Vehicle Discount £0.00 / week
Plug-in Hybrid (PHEV) (Mercedes E300e) Subject to daily £15 fee in central zone Up to £75–£105 / week
Self-Charging Hybrid (Toyota Corolla Estate) Subject to daily £15 fee in central zone Up to £75–£105 / week

Note: Ride-hailing platforms like Uber and Bolt automatically add a Congestion Charge surcharge to passenger fares when a ride begins or ends inside the CCZ. However, driving through the zone without an active passenger journey remains the driver's expense.

2. Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) Regulations

The Ultra Low Emission Zone applies 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year across all London boroughs within the M25 boundary. Non-compliant vehicles face a daily fee of £12.50.

Cleaner Vehicle Discount (CVD) Registration

To enjoy exemption from the central Congestion Charge, 100% electric zero-emission vehicles (such as our Tesla Model 3 or MG5 EV) must be registered on the official TfL Cleaner Vehicle Discount portal. Registration costs £10 annually per vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do PCO drivers pay the London Congestion Charge?

PCO drivers in non-EV vehicles pay the £15 daily fee when operating in central London during operational hours. 100% electric vehicles are exempt via the Cleaner Vehicle Discount.

Are hybrid cars exempt from the Congestion Charge?

No. Standard hybrids and plug-in hybrids are subject to the daily Congestion Charge. Only 100% battery-electric zero-emission vehicles qualify for exemption.

Are all Virtual Car Hire vehicles ULEZ compliant?

Yes. 100% of the Virtual Car Hire fleet meets ULEZ standards, ensuring you never pay daily ULEZ charges anywhere in Greater London.
