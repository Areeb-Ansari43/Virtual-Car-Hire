FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What documents do I need to hire a PCO car?
You'll need your UK driving licence, TfL PHV badge, proof of address, and National Insurance number. We handle the platform uploads to Uber, Bolt and Ola for you.
Is insurance included in the weekly rate?
Yes — comprehensive motor trade insurance is fully included along with servicing, MOT, and roadside breakdown cover.
Can I use the car on Uber, Bolt and Ola?
Absolutely. Every vehicle is TfL-licensed and PCO-ready. We'll upload your documents to all major platforms.
How quickly can I get on the road?
Most drivers collect and start working the same day, once documents are verified and the agreement is signed.
Do you offer electric or hybrid cars?
Yes — our fleet includes a growing range of full electric (Tesla Model 3, Mercedes EQE, EQS) and hybrid options (Toyota Corolla Estate, Prius, Hyundai IONIQ and more).
What happens if the car breaks down?
Full roadside breakdown assistance is included in every hire, so you're never left stranded.
