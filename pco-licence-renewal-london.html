How to Renew Your PCO Licence in London (2026)

Your TfL private hire driver licence lasts up to three years. Renewing it on time is simple if you start early and have the right documents ready. This guide walks you through it.

Last reviewed: November 2026 7 min read Written by the Virtual Car Hire team

When to start your renewal

TfL asks drivers to submit renewals as early as possible, because checks outside TfL's control can slow things down. In practice, most drivers start around three to four months before their licence expires, when TfL's reminder arrives.

Never drive on an expired licence

If your licence runs out before the renewal is granted, you must stop taking private hire work. Driving without a valid licence is an offence and can invalidate your insurance. Start early so you never have to choose between waiting and working.

What you need to renew

  • An active DBS Update Service subscription. TfL will not renew your licence without one, so check it has not lapsed.
  • A tax check code from HMRC. You need a new one each time you renew. Codes are valid for 120 days, so do not get it too early.
  • A medical, if one is due. TfL tells you when a new medical is needed. Book your GP or a private doctor early, as appointments can take weeks.
  • Your photocard driving licence and proof of address. Make sure your address matches across your documents.
  • Any outstanding assessments. If you have not yet passed the SERU or English language requirements TfL has set for you, book them now.

Step by step

Step 1

Check your DBS Update Service

Log in and make sure your subscription is active and paid up.

Step 2

Get your HMRC tax check code

Request it online through your Government Gateway account, close to when you submit.

Step 3

Submit your renewal to TfL

Apply online with your documents and pay the application fee.

Step 4

Complete anything TfL asks for

Medical, assessments or extra documents. Reply quickly to avoid delays.

Step 5

Pay the grant fee and receive your licence

Keep your badge visible and update your operator apps with the new expiry date.

If your renewal is delayed

TfL has had backlogs in recent years. If you applied on time and your renewal is still being processed, check TfL's latest licensing notices: at times TfL has offered short-term licences to existing drivers who meet set criteria. Keep proof that you applied, and contact TfL's licensing team if you have heard nothing.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I renew my PCO licence?

TfL asks drivers to apply as early as possible. Most drivers start about three to four months before expiry.

How much does it cost to renew a PCO licence in 2026?

From 1 June 2026, TfL charges a £138 application fee and a £205 grant fee, £343 in total, plus any medical or DBS costs.

Do I need a new DBS check to renew?

You need an active DBS Update Service subscription. TfL will not renew your licence without one.

Can I keep driving while my renewal is processed?

Only while your current licence is valid. Once it expires you must stop taking work until the renewal is granted, unless TfL has issued you a short-term licence.
