TfL Private Hire Vehicle Rules 2026: Zero Emission and Age Limits

Which cars can be used for private hire in London is set by TfL, and the rules keep moving towards electric. Here is where things stand in 2026, and what is being proposed next.

Last reviewed: November 2026 8 min read Written by the Virtual Car Hire team

Zero emission capable (ZEC) for first-time licences

The rule since January 2023

Since 1 January 2023, every private hire vehicle licensed by TfL for the first time must be zero emission capable (ZEC) and meet the Euro 6 emissions standard. Fully electric and hydrogen cars meet it. A plug-in hybrid can qualify too: TfL's criteria when the rule was announced included CO2 emissions of no more than 50g/km and at least 10 miles of zero-emission range.

TfL asks for evidence such as the V5C showing CO2 emissions and the Euro standard. If you cannot provide it, the application can be refused.

Age limits

TfL's guidance says vehicles already licensed must be no older than 10 years when they are relicensed. Check the current rules on TfL's emissions standards page before buying an older car, as the details can change.

What is coming next

In October 2026 TfL consulted on making all newly licensed private hire vehicles fully zero emission from July 2028, with wheelchair-accessible vehicles following from 2030. These are proposals, not final rules. If they go ahead, a plug-in hybrid licensed before then could carry on, but new licences would need a fully electric car.

ULEZ and the Congestion Charge

  • ULEZ: covers all London boroughs, 24 hours a day. Non-compliant cars pay £12.50 a day. Cars meeting the private hire rules above are compliant.
  • Congestion Charge: £18 a day in central London. Since 2 January 2026, fully electric cars on TfL Auto Pay get a 25% discount (£13.50). The old free Cleaner Vehicle Discount ended on 25 December 2025. Full details are in our Congestion Charge guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can a hybrid be licensed as a PHV in London?

A plug-in hybrid can, if it meets TfL's zero emission capable criteria. A self-charging hybrid cannot be licensed for the first time, though some already licensed before 2023 can continue to be relicensed.

What is the age limit for a PCO car in London?

TfL guidance says licensed vehicles must be no older than 10 years at relicensing. Check TfL for the current rules.

Will all new PCO cars have to be electric?

TfL has proposed that all newly licensed private hire vehicles be fully zero emission from July 2028. This was out for consultation in October 2026 and is not yet final.

Do electric PCO cars pay the Congestion Charge?

Yes, since 25 December 2025. Fully electric cars on Auto Pay pay £13.50 a day instead of £18.
