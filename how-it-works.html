How Virtual Car Hire Works

A step-by-step guide to renting a TfL-licensed PCO vehicle in London and Luton. Transparent pricing, full insurance, and same-day collection.

Step 1

Choose Your Vehicle

Explore our wide selection of TfL-licensed executive saloons, hybrids, electric vehicles, and 7-seater MPVs. Whether you drive for Uber X, Uber Green, Uber Exec, Bolt, or Ola, we have vehicles ready for immediate hire.

All vehicles in our fleet are fully compliant with Transport for London (TfL) regulations and suitable for rideshare operations.
Step 2

Check the Document Requirements

Before collecting your vehicle, ensure you have the required documentation ready for verification:

  • UK Driving Licence (held for 1+ year)
  • TfL Private Hire Driver Licence (PCO Badge + Paper Licence)
  • Proof of Address (utility bill/bank statement within 3 months)
  • National Insurance Number / DVLA Share Code
Step 3

Submit Your Application

Submit your document copies via our website contact form, WhatsApp, or email. Our team will quickly verify your details and prepare your vehicle agreement.

Step 4

Approval & Document Uploads

Once approved, we will upload the vehicle documents (V5 logbook, TfL PHV licence, and Hire Insurance Certificate) directly to your Uber, Bolt, or Ola driver accounts to ensure seamless activation.

Step 5

Payment & Deposit

Before vehicle collection, settle the refundable security deposit and initial weekly rental amount. All rental terms, payment dates, and deposit statuses are clearly displayed inside your Virtual Car Hire Customer Portal.

Step 6

Vehicle Collection

Collect your vehicle from our operational base in Hounslow, West London. Perform a joint walk-around inspection with our staff, receive the vehicle keys, and hit the road.

Step 7

During Your Rental

Enjoy hassle-free driving with fully included routine servicing, MOT maintenance, and roadside breakdown assistance. Submit your monthly odometer reading easily online via your customer portal.

Step 8

Customer Portal & Support

Manage your rental 24/7 with the Virtual Car Hire Customer Portal. Download statements/invoices, check weekly payments, access documents, and contact roadside support whenever you need assistance.
