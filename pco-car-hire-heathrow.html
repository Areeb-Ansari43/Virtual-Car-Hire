Collecting your car near Heathrow

Every car is collected from our depot at The Vista Centre, 50 Salisbury Road, Cranford, Hounslow TW4 6JQ. It is our only base, and it sits a short drive from Heathrow, so you can be on airport work soon after you collect.

Collections are booked in advance, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm. Most drivers collect the same day once documents are checked and the agreement is signed.

Heathrow FAQ

Questions from Heathrow drivers

Where do I collect my PCO car for Heathrow work?
From our depot at The Vista Centre, 50 Salisbury Road, Cranford, Hounslow TW4 6JQ. It is a short drive from Heathrow Airport. Collections are booked in advance, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm.
Which cars suit Heathrow airport runs?
Estates like the Toyota Corolla and Auris carry plenty of luggage. For UberXL and group transfers there are 7 to 9-seat options: the Mercedes Vito, Mercedes V-Class, Ford Tourneo Custom Titanium and Hyundai Santa Fe.
Can I use the cars on Uber, Bolt and Ola?
Yes. Every car is TfL licensed and PCO ready, and we upload your vehicle documents to Uber, Bolt and Ola for you.
What is included in the weekly price?
Insurance, servicing, MOT and roadside breakdown cover are included, with 5,000 miles a month.
When do you reply to enquiries?
We reply within 24 hours on weekdays, Monday to Friday. Messages sent at the weekend are answered on Monday.
